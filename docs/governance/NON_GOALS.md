# Non-Goals of HADRA-PRIME (ADRAE)

This document defines what the system is explicitly NOT designed to do.

These are permanent exclusions.

---

## Quiet Wake Is NOT:
- A throttled Wake
- A low-power thinking mode
- Background cognition
- Idle curiosity
- A failure state

Quiet Wake is intentional restraint.

---

## Sleep Is NOT:
- Shutdown
- Hibernation
- Resource exhaustion response
- Avoidance of responsibility
- A shortcut to skip arbitration

Sleep is protected consolidation.

---

## Wake Is NOT:
- Always-on cognition
- Background thinking
- A default state
- A reward
- A place for unbounded exploration

Wake is temporary and scoped.

---

## Learning Is NOT:
- Continuous
- Unbounded
- Self-directed without permission
- A replacement for governance

Learning must be earned, bounded, and reviewed.

---

## Guardians Are NOT:
- Controllers
- Executors
- Commanders
- Autonomous decision-makers

Guardians observe and signal only.

---

## Telemetry Is NOT:
- Introspection
- Surveillance
- Optimization input
- Feedback into cognition

Telemetry observes, then gets out of the way.

---

## The System Is NOT:
- Designed to maximize activity
- Designed to be impressive
- Designed to fill silence
- Designed to compete with humans
- Designed to replace agency

The system exists to protect continuity and enable intentional action.
