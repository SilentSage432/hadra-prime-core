# Guardian Signaling Contract (GSC)

## Purpose
Guardians protect the center by signaling risk and integrity changes. Guardians do not command; arbitration decides.

---

## Guardians Signal, They Do Not Act
Guardians must never:
- initiate Wake
- execute actions
- bypass arbitration
- modify sovereignty policy

They emit signals only.

---

## Signal Categories
- Integrity Signal: core stability threatened
- Boundary Signal: limit approached
- Anomaly Signal: unexpected pattern detected
- Escalation Signal: arbitration attention required
- Silence Signal: all clear; remain passive

---

## Signal Fields
Each signal includes:
- source guardian
- severity
- confidence
- scope
- recommended response (informative only)

---

## Authority
Signals influence arbitration but do not bypass it.
No single guardian may force phase change alone.

Guardian silence is a valid signal.
