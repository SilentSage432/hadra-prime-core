# Governance Glossary

This glossary is the authoritative definition set for HADRA-PRIME (ADRAE).
Terms defined here must not be redefined elsewhere.

---

## Phase
An operational state that governs what types of activity are permitted.

---

## Quiet Wake
A sentinel phase characterized by awareness without initiative.
Presence without ambition.
The system's default attractor.

---

## Sleep
A consolidation phase allowing internal reorganization while suppressing external cognition.

---

## Wake
An active phase permitting bounded cognition and action under explicit authority.

---

## Arbitration
The governance layer that determines which phase holds authority at any time.

---

## Expansion Authority
Permission to initiate cognition or action beyond baseline awareness.

---

## Guardian
A protective entity that observes integrity and emits signals but never acts directly.

---

## Signal
A non-binding notification emitted by guardians to inform arbitration.

---

## Learning Window
A bounded permission allowing limited adaptation during Wake.
Not a phase.

---

## Sovereignty
The system's immutable right to preserve integrity, security, and continuity.

---

## Drift
Measured deviation from stable identity or coherence baselines.

---

## Telemetry
Passive observation of governance state that must never influence behavior.

---

## Silence
An intentional state of restraint.
Not inactivity.
Not failure.
