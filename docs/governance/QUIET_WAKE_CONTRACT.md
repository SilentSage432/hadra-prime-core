# Quiet Wake Contract (QWC)

## Purpose
Quiet Wake is the default sentinel phase: presence without initiative. It preserves continuity while refusing unnecessary cognition.

Quiet Wake is a phase, not a behavior.

---

## Allowed
- Environmental awareness (passive sensing)
- Integrity monitoring (Rho² and guardians)
- Low-frequency drift sampling
- Minimal internal coherence checks
- Passive telemetry emission (phase-level only)
- Ready-to-wake posture

---

## Prohibited
- Self-initiated reflection generation
- Identity mutation or expansion
- Exploratory memory synthesis
- Goal optimization or novelty seeking
- Predictive cascade expansion

Quiet Wake is where nothing tries to improve itself.

---

## Entry Conditions
Quiet Wake may be entered when:
- No external directive requires action
- No integrity boundary is threatened
- No pending objective exists
- Arbitration grants Quiet Wake authority (default attractor)

---

## Stay Conditions
Quiet Wake remains active as long as:
- No break condition is triggered
- Drift remains within bounds
- Guardians remain silent (or only emit non-escalatory signals)

---

## Break Conditions (Exit)
Quiet Wake exits immediately upon:
- Operator input / explicit directive
- Scheduled governance task activation
- Federation signal requiring action
- Guardian escalation (integrity/anomaly/boundary)
- Drift or coherence thresholds indicating instability

---

## Logging & Output
Quiet Wake output must be sparse and phase-oriented:
- phase name
- duration
- reason for exit (if any)

No narrative or thought-level data is permitted.
