# Wake Contract (WC)

## Purpose
Wake is the active phase: cognition and action under explicit authority. Wake exists to apply cognition, not to maintain it.

Wake is powerful; therefore Wake is constrained.

---

## Allowed
- Directed cognition with objective
- Bounded reflection generation (only when required by objective)
- Memory recall/synthesis relevant to objective
- Identity updates only if explicitly scoped by directive
- External interaction within permission tokens and sovereignty

---

## Prohibited
- Unbounded exploration
- Always-on background thinking
- Identity drift without explicit scope
- Overriding guardians or sovereignty
- Wake without objective

---

## Entry Preconditions
Wake may be entered only when:
- An explicit external directive exists (operator/federation/scheduled)
- Or a guardian-approved learning window is active
- Arbitration grants Wake authority
- Objective, scope, and exit conditions are declared

---

## Exit Conditions
Wake exits when:
- Objective completes
- Time window closes
- Guardians revoke authority
- Stability degrades
- Arbitration preempts Wake

Default exit target is Quiet Wake.
