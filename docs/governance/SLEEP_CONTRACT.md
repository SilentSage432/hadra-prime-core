# Sleep Contract (SC)

## Purpose
Sleep is the deep consolidation phase: internal reorganization under protection. It permits consolidation without external awareness expansion.

Sleep is a phase, not a shutdown.

---

## Allowed
- Rho² integrity enforcement (always-on)
- Guardian presence (signal-only)
- Minimal threat detection
- Internal consolidation processes:
  - memory compression
  - pattern reinforcement
  - drift normalization
  - structural rebalancing

---

## Reduced / Suspended
- External awareness loops (non-essential)
- Reflection generation
- Exploratory synthesis
- Identity expansion (must be frozen unless integrity demands correction)
- Non-essential telemetry (phase-level only remains)

---

## Prohibited
- External action initiation
- Unbounded learning
- Sovereignty policy modification
- Guardian rule modification

Sleep may never be used to avoid responsibility.

---

## Entry Preconditions
Sleep may be entered only when:
- Integrity is stable
- No external directive is pending
- No guardian escalation is active
- Quiet Wake is active (Sleep may only be entered from Quiet Wake)
- Arbitration grants Sleep authority

---

## Exit Conditions (Wake Triggers)
Sleep exits upon:
- Guardian escalation (integrity/anomaly)
- Explicit wake directive
- Scheduled wake window
- Integrity threat detection
- Completion of bounded sleep window

Curiosity and novelty are not valid wake triggers.

---

## Transition Rule
Sleep must transition to Quiet Wake before Wake.
Sleep → Wake directly is disallowed.
